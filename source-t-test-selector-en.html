Which t-test does
my study need?
Just check who you're comparing.
1 One-sample You vs a benchmark
"Our stress is M = 3.4; the national average is 3.0. Are we different?"
2 Independent Two separate groups
"Men average 2.1 hours of SNS a day, women 2.9. Real gap or noise?"
3 Paired Same people, twice
"Stress fell from M = 3.8 to 3.1. Did the program work?"
Different people → 2  ·  Same people twice → 3
